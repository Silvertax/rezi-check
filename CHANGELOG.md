# Changelog

## 1.6

### What's new

- **New design:** rounded cards, pill buttons and a new app icon. The inspection stamp stays.
- **Theme:** under Settings → Theme choose automatic (like the phone), light or dark.
- **OLED mode:** makes the dark theme pure black. The dark theme itself is now a soft graphite; switch on OLED mode for the pure black look of earlier versions.
- Settings are grouped into cards, with switches instead of checkboxes.

## 1.5

### What's new

- **Settings tab:** settings now have their own tab, with buttons to request a feature or report a bug.
- **Sharing:** when you share a product from the Amazon app, Rezi-Check asks whether to check quickly or thoroughly. Under Settings → When sharing you can make it start right away instead.
- **Faster:** shared links are checked faster.
- **More honest grades:** grade A is only given when at least 20 reviews were checked; with fewer than 12 reviews the grade is marked as preliminary.

## 1.4

### What's new

- **Update notice:** the app now tells you when a new version is available. It asks GitHub at most once a day; no personal data is sent. You can turn it off under Settings → Check for updates.
- **Report problem:** opens a GitHub issue with the diagnostics already filled in.
- **New check:** reviews that are about a different product – a sign of taken-over reviews.
- **New check:** brand names that look like random letters (a weak hint).

## 1.3

### What's new

- New info button (“i”) next to the Amazon sign-in: explains why signing in gives a more reliable result and what happens to your data.

## 1.2

### What's new

- Dark mode now uses a pure black background (#000000) – easy on the battery with OLED screens.

## 1.1

### What's new

- Rezi-Check is now available in English.
- The app automatically starts in your phone’s language. You can change it under Settings → Language (on Android 13 and later also under “App languages” in the Android settings).
- New under Settings → Amazon store: for “Thorough” you can now sign in to Amazon.com, .co.uk, .fr, .it, .es and other stores.

## 1.0

First version of Rezi-Check (German interface; English was added in 1.1).

### Features

- Check Amazon links via “Share” from the Amazon app or by pasting them
- Grade from A to F with reasons and quotes from the reviews as evidence
- “Quick” (product page only, no sign-in) and “Thorough” (with Amazon sign-in, up to 50 more reviews)
- History, light and dark mode – no server, no tracking, no ads

The result is an estimate based on typical patterns, not proof.
